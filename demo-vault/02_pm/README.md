# Marcus Chen — Anchor

Marcus is a senior PM at **Anchor**, a B2B project-management SaaS for software teams ($14K MRR average account, mid-market focus). He's been there 18 months. Owns the onboarding + analytics surfaces.

## Voice

- Direct, structured. Uses headers and bullet lists heavily.
- Quantifies when possible. "Churn dropped 12%" not "churn dropped a lot".
- Meeting notes are short. Decisions get their own doc.
- PRDs follow a template — context, problem, success metrics, scope, risks.

## Notes in this folder

- `PRDs/` — product requirement docs (in-flight specs)
- `Meetings/` — meeting notes (sync, customer, exec)
- `Customers/` — account notes for at-risk / expansion targets
- `Inbox.md` — loose threads, links to triage

## Best for screenshots of

- **Curtis Agent** — `create_note` from a meeting transcript; `edit_note` to update a PRD
- **@-mention** — attach meeting notes as context, ask "what are the open questions?"
- **Slash commands** — `/note` to save an AI summary into the vault
- **Cross-conversation search** — find where a feature was last discussed

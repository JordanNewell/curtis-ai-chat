# Vector Mag — Client Brief

## The publication

Vector Mag is a monthly tech-and-culture publication. Long-form essays, personal-voice angle, no news-cycle churn. Reads like a more literary Stratechery. Audience: senior operators, founders, editors, ~14K subscribers.

## Editor

**Casey Park.** Senior editor. Prefers email for pitches, responds within a week. Doesn't like phone calls. Has good taste, will push for sharper angles. Once killed a 2000-word piece I'd written because "you're being too nice to the subject — what do you actually think?"

Take Casey's edits seriously. They're usually right.

## Voice

- First-person singular. Even in reported pieces, presence of the narrator is expected.
- Concrete > abstract. Don't say "the industry shifted." Say "by the third quarter, half my clients had frozen hires."
- Present tense for scene work, past tense for reflection.
- No exclamation points. Ever. (Casey's only hard rule.)

## Pitch format

Subject line: `PITCH: [working title] — [one-line hook]`

Body:
1. **Hook** — 2 sentences max. Why this, why now.
2. **Angle** — what's the personal stake? Why are you the one to write it?
3. **Sources** — who will you talk to? (Vector prefers 2-3 interviews even for personal essays.)
4. **Length** — usually 1800-2200 words.
5. **Timing** — when can you deliver?

Casey usually responds within 4 days. If you don't hear back in a week, ping once. After that, move on.

## Rate + terms

- **$0.85/word** for the monthly column
- Kill fee: 25%
- Exclusivity: 30 days from publication
- Rights: First North American serial + non-exclusive archive

## Workflow

1. Pitch → Casey approves (or sends back for sharpening)
2. Draft 1 → due 7 days before pub date
3. Casey's edit pass → 2-3 days
4. Draft 2 → due 2 days before pub date
5. Copyedit → 1 day
6. Publication

The rhythm is monthly, so it's tight. Don't pitch if you can't hit the windows.

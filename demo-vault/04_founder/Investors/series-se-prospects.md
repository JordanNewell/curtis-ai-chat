# Series Seed prospects

## Context

I haven't decided whether to raise. Pylon is at $8K MRR, 23 paying customers, 80% gross margin. Growing ~12% MoM. If I don't raise, I can stay solo + 1 part-time contractor for ~18 more months before I need to either grow into hiring or take a salary cut.

If I raise $1.5M at a $10-12M post, I can hire 2 engineers + 1 GTM, get to $50K MRR in 12 months, and either raise a larger A or bootstrap from there.

The math says raise. The instinct says don't. This doc tracks both.

## Why raise

- **Velocity.** 12% MoM is fine but not breakout. Two engineers would let me ship the language coverage I keep deferring.
- **Network.** Right investor opens doors to design partners. I'm currently cold-emailing; that doesn't scale.
- **Optionality.** Even if I don't spend it, having 18 months of runway after a raise lets me say no to bad deals, bad customers, bad acquisitions.

## Why not raise

- **Dilution.** 12-15% at seed, plus the option pool shuffle, plus the future A. At exit I'd own ~50% of what I'd have owned bootstrapping.
- **Board.** Even a friendly seed investor wants monthly updates, wants input on hires, wants to know why churn ticked up. I move faster without that loop.
- **Pressure to spend.** Money raised is money expected to be deployed. Hard to keep the bootstrapper discipline.
- **Time.** A raise takes 3-4 months of my time. That's 3-4 months of not building.

## Decision criteria

I'll pitch if and only if:
1. Tier 1 list above produces at least 2 warm conversations
2. I can articulate "why now" without hand-waving
3. I have at least 1 design partner willing to give a reference
4. My gut says yes by August 15

If any of those fail, defer to Q1 2027 and bootstrap through the holidays.
